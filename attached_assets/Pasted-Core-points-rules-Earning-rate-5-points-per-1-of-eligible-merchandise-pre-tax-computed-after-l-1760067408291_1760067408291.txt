Core points rules

Earning rate: 5 points per $1 of eligible merchandise (pre-tax), computed after line-item promos are applied.

Redemption value: 100 points = $1 off (whole-dollar steps).

Per-trip cap: $10 max via points per transaction.

Spend gate: Points redemption is limited by spend:
max_by_spend = floor(eligible_subtotal_after_item_promos / 20) dollars.
max_by_points = floor(points_balance / 100) dollars.
redeem_dollars = min(max_by_spend, max_by_points, 10)
points_to_use = redeem_dollars * 100

Exclusions (earn/redemption eligibility)

Lottery: ❌ excluded (does not earn; not counted toward points redemption gate).

Tobacco & Alcohol: ✅ included (earns and counts toward redemption gate).

(You can still block specific SKUs later; this is the default.)

Promotions + points (stacking priority)

Yes, customers can use BOTH a promotion and points in the same sale.

Do not double-discount a line that already has a POS-applied promotion:

If the POS marks a line as already discounted or not discountable, do not apply an additional loyalty line promo to that line.

Still allow a basket-level points discount on the post-promo subtotal.

Order of operations:

Apply item-level promos (e.g., Coffee at $0.99, 2-for deals).

Recompute eligible subtotal (excluding lottery).

Offer points redemption as a ticket-level amountOff (optional prompt).

Earn points on the post-promo eligible subtotal (pre-tax).

Rounding & edge cases

Earning: use floor on dollars×5 (e.g., $12.79 → 63 pts).

Redemption: whole dollars only ($1, $2, …); never exceed transaction total.

No negative lines: cap line discounts at line price; cap basket discount at basket total.

Returns/voids:

If items are returned, subtract earned points tied to those items.

If points were redeemed, re-credit points at 100 pts per $1 refunded.

Receipt & UX

Prompt for points by default: “Redeem $X for Y pts?” (site toggle to auto-apply if desired).

Print lines like:

Coffee Club $0.99 -$0.80

Points Redemption -$2.00

Earned 63 pts • New balance: 1,180 pts

Example (quick sanity check)

Cart after item promos (incl. tobacco/alcohol, excl. lottery): $47.18

Balance: 380 pts

max_by_spend = floor(47.18/20) = 2 → $2

max_by_points = floor(380/100) = 3 → $3

Cap = $10 → redeem_dollars = min(2, 3, 10) = 2

Apply $2 ticket-level discount, use 200 pts; earn on $47.18 → 235 pts.
