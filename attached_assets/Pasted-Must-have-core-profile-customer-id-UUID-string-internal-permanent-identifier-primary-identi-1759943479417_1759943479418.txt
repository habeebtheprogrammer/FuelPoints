Must-have (core profile)

customer_id (UUID string) – internal, permanent identifier

primary_identifier (string) – what they present at the lane (e.g., phone or barcode)

id_type (enum: phone, barcode, email, external_id)

first_name (string)

last_name (string)

email (string; validated)

phone_e164 (string; e.g., +12025550123)

status (enum: active, blocked, merged)

created_at, updated_at (timestamps)

Loyalty account (current state only)

loyalty_account_id (UUID string)

points_balance (int) – available points

lifetime_points_earned (int)

lifetime_points_redeemed (int)

last_earn_at, last_redeem_at (timestamps)

Identifiers (if you support more than one handle)

identifiers (array of objects) with:

id_type (phone|barcode|email|external_id)

id_value (string, normalized; keep leading zeros)

is_primary (bool)

status (active|revoked)

Consents & comms (keep it simple)

consents:

marketing_email (bool)

marketing_sms (bool)

source (e.g., web_form, pos)

updated_at (timestamp)

Preferences (optional but useful)

language (e.g., en, es)

timezone (IANA string, e.g., America/New_York)

receipt_pref (none|email|sms)

Light demographics (only what you use)

birth_mmdd (string, MM-DD; month/day only for birthday rewards)

zip_code (string; optional for geo promos)

Addresses (optional)

address:

line1, line2, city, state, postal, country

Store/site linkage (optional)

home_site_id (string; your PDI site id)

pos_store_id (string; last seen POS site)

Practical guardrails

Strings for IDs (PDI/pos store ids, barcodes) to preserve leading zeros.

Normalize phone to E.164; also consider storing a hashed_phone for non-PII lookups.

Unique constraint on active identifiers: (id_type, id_value, status='active').

PII hygiene: encrypt email, phone_e164 at rest; mask in logs.

Start lean (core + points). You can add preferences/consents later without breaking anything.
